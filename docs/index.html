The Reality On Ground

Help exists. People just can’t reach it.

During our Ashadhi Wari field visit in Alandi and Pandharpur, we witnessed the disconnect firsthand: aid stations and volunteers are all around, yet lost pilgrims, dehydrated elders, and medical cases struggle to reach them across congested routes.

👥

Overcrowding

Massive gatherings overwhelm narrow alleys and ghat steps. Navigating through sea of people makes physical searching for services exhausting and risky.

🧭

People get separated

Family members, especially elderly pilgrims without modern smartphones, lose their group in seconds with no way to convey their location or find help.

📶

Poor connectivity

Cellular networks jam in dense crowds; many elders have no smartphone. When towers fail, conventional apps and internet services become unreachable.

⏱️

Delayed medical help

During emergencies, ambulances cannot navigate blocked roads. Without nearby verified volunteer coordination, critical minutes are lost.

Simple & Accessible

How SangamIQ works in 3 steps

Engineered for everyone from rural senior citizens to district administration control rooms.

1

Ask in your own way

Tap a giant high-contrast button, speak naturally in Marathi or Hindi via voice AI, or call our toll-free line from any basic keypad phone. No complicated smartphone apps needed.

2

AI finds the nearest help

Our matching engine routes requests to verified, nearby volunteers based on distance, skill fit, and current workload—or guides you to the nearest medical camp, water, food, toilet, help desk, or transport.

3

Organisers see the full picture

Command centers monitor a live crowd density heatmap, review 15-minute predictive surge alerts, manage volunteer capacity, and broadcast multilingual emergency warnings.
